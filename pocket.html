MADHOUSE POCKET

Everyone knows it's a game. We say so. — the honest game

📜 The rules — read first, all of them, up front

  1. Fixed supply. 1,000,000 Dots exist. No printing. Ever. (Dots are also called Spots — like spots of paint on the canvas.)
  2. Nothing is created. Every Dot you receive comes from The Pool. When the Pool is empty, no more grants — the game plays on with what's circulating.
  3. Welcome Dots. Every new arrival gets 100 Dots from the Pool. Free. No catch.
  4. The Price List is the reference. Give the thing away, or ask less — wonderful. Ask the reference — fine. Ask more — you keep the reference; the excess becomes The Donation.
  5. The Donation flows back into the Pool, funding future Welcome Dots. The gouge feeds the generous.
  6. Conservation, always: Pool + all Pockets + Donations = fixed supply. Watch it hold, live, below.

Demonstration figures — adjustable. This page is a working sketch of the game loop, not a live service.
