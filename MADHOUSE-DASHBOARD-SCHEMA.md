## Guardrails

- No money, no price, no promise, no debt in the young door. Parental permission openly.
- No phone number, no biometrics, no government registration to enter.
- Map pins at city level only, opt-in, never exact tracking, never a master list of who entered.
- No coin project, no crypto asset offering, no gambling, no Stars giveaways.
- Every card has a next tap. No dead ends.
- Contributors are vetted: introduce first, real first contribution, vouching/recognition, proven before a full code handoff.
