# The MADHOUSE Manifesto

*Why we're building this. One page. Read it first.*

People don't need another app. They need each other.

MADHOUSE is a meeting engine disguised as a game. Young people walk out of the
systems that were built to harvest them, and they meet — in person, on purpose,
as independent sovereigns with a collective mindset. Generosity. Kindness.
Enlightened self-interest. The reverse pyramid: the ones at the top serve, the
clan grows wider.

**The Dots aren't the point — they're the excuse.** The excuse to knock on a
stranger's door. To run an errand together. To trade a skill for a story. To
publish a wish and watch strangers carry it. To be seen and be heard and be
free.

We took the best of every system we could find and left their failure modes
behind. The creator loop of Roblox without the off-ramp. The player shops of
Minecraft without the infinite buy-order. The conservation guarantees of Libra
without the regulators' kill-shot. The fungibility of Monero — every Dot equal,
your pocket no one's business. The feeless instant payments Bitcoin Cash
dreamed of, on rails so boring they just work. Payments as native web actions,
the way the web always promised.

And we run it like the Peace Corps meets the Boy Scouts. Wholesome on purpose.
No loot boxes. No gacha. No pay-to-win. No surveillance — admin sees aggregates
only, never your pocket. No one under sixteen walks into anything real without
gates a parent could inspect.

**Nothing is real until it's real.** We show the work: 614 tests green, the
site live, the bot answering, the ledger conserved to the Dot. What's real is
real and what's proposed is proposed, and we say which — in every document, out
loud. That's the deal we offer every builder who joins: no hype, no vapor, the
honest state of the machine.

The door ritual says it all. You arrive, you get 100 Dots, and you pass 5 to
the next newcomer with your name attached. Or you keep all 100 — no judgment.
The system never requires generosity. The culture unmistakably rewards it.

We need help. Game designers. Payment architects. Mesh engineers. Rule-makers.
Artists, writers, musicians. Playtesters. Seeders. People who read all this and
feel the click.

Help, help, help — we mean it.

It's a game of life — with happiness for all.

Jesters welcome. Playful children and adults welcome.

The door is open. Walk through it.

Your cosmic energy and family await.
