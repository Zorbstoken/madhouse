# The Creed — who we are

*His words, dictated 2026-10-17. Shaped only with line breaks.*

We are a group of broad minded individuals beings whom promote freedom of
expression through trade and commerce, through generosity of spirit and
fairness to all.

Through gifting.
Through purpose.
Through positive intentions.
Through unlimited possibilities —
unformed possibilities drawn forth and shared from a well spring of knowledge,
and unlimited possibilities, potential realized.

Through shared experiences —
enlightened self-awareness and interests, in co-operation with others of same
similar nature,
evoked.

Maintaining individualized being, in cooperation with the collective.

Entourage —
entities of eternity.

Assisting self and others through good works and kindness.
